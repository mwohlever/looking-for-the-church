Looking for the Church?

Lately we've been wondering what it could look like to join with others that want to intentionally follow Jesus together in Columbus, Ohio. We don’t want to create a Sunday service or Christian event, simply BE The Church, together with others that are trying to do the same.

We want to share life, love our neighbors, serve the people around us, enjoy food, pray together, and be transformed by Jesus — but we're not interested in “planting” a new church, joining an organized religion, or over-committing ourselves in a way that doesn’t allow for us to truly invest in our community. We’ve considered these options or tried them out and again and again we sense that it’s just not what God is asking us to do.

We’re curious about if others in Columbus are sensing the same nudgings of the Spirit of God and want to gather together. If you’ve been looking for a place to try out a diverse expression of The Church and want to try that within a community of people seeking Jesus, let us know. We’d love to hear your story!
