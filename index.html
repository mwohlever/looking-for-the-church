Looking for the Church?

My wife and I live in Columbus, Ohio, and lately we've been wondering what it might look like to find other people who want to follow Jesus together without making a Sunday service or church event the center of it. We'd love the focus to be sharing life, loving our neighbors, serving people around us, eating together, praying together — but we're honestly not 100% sure what that exactly looks like. We're not planting a church and we don't have a model we're trying to build. We're mostly curious whether there are other people around Columbus wondering about the same things. If you are, we'd love to hear from you.
